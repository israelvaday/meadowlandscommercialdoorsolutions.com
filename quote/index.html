Common questions

Questions about commercial door services across Jersey City & North Jersey

Straightforward answers from Meadowlands Commercial Door Solutions about commercial doors in Jersey City, Hudson County & the Meadowlands. Tap a question to expand.

A clear quote lists the door, frame, hardware, weatherstripping, any fire rating, and framing work. Damage or building repairs that are not part of the door job should be called out before work starts.

Many Hudson County offices, shops, and mixed-use buildings need labeled fire doors in stairs, corridors, and fire-separation walls. The quote should list the required label, a self-closing closer, and smoke seals when those parts are part of the job.

Hardware depends on how heavy the door is, how often it is used, and how secure the opening needs to be. Locks, closers, hinges, and exit hardware should match the door and the building — not a one-size-fits-all kit.

A door that binds, sags, or will not latch is often a frame, hinge, or strike problem — not a ruined door. Repair can restore smooth, secure operation and keep the existing door when that is the better value.

We measure the opening width and height, plus the swing, closer, and hardware clearances. Storefronts and corridor doors usually need a site visit before we order the door.

River wind, salt air, and heavy foot traffic all wear doors harder. We match the door and hardware to the building — storefront, restaurant, office, or lobby — instead of treating every opening the same.

The finish should include hauling away the old door, testing locks and closers, and walking the job with you against the written quote.
